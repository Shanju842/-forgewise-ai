# Problem Statement

## Selected challenge

**Theme:** Industrial Manufacturing  
**Problem statement:** 4.1 – Small factories overpay for raw material and quote orders at a loss.

## Context

Small manufacturing businesses may rely on informal supplier communications, manually maintained inventory records and spreadsheets to prepare quotations. Supplier prices alone do not show the full cost of procurement: freight, delivery timing, quality/specification fit, minimum order quantities and stock already on hand can all affect the decision.

Customer quotations can also omit material wastage, setup time, machine hours, labour, electricity, rejection/rework and overhead allocation. If these costs are underestimated, a factory may accept an order that does not meet its required margin or may lose money.

## Users

- Owners and managers of small manufacturing units
- Procurement and stores staff
- Production supervisors
- Estimation and quotation staff

## Core needs

1. Understand customer RFQs and identify missing specifications.
2. Compare supplier quotations on a like-for-like basis.
3. Account for inventory before recommending a purchase.
4. Estimate production cost using explicit assumptions.
5. Identify orders that may be loss-making.
6. Keep the factory owner in control of financial commitments.

## Problem boundaries

ForgeWise AI is intended as a decision-support tool. It does not guarantee supplier prices, material quality, delivery, manufacturing feasibility or profitability. Recommendations depend on the completeness and accuracy of the factory's data and must be reviewed by a responsible person.
